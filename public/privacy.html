Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

TheModelSays ("we", "our", or "the site") is a free AI-powered sports prediction tool for the 2026 FIFA World Cup. This page explains how we handle information when you use our website at themodelsays.com.

Information We Collect

We do not require you to create an account or provide any personal information to use TheModelSays. The site does not directly collect names, email addresses, or payment information.

When you visit our site, standard technical information may be processed, including:

  • Your IP address (processed by our hosting provider, Vercel)
  • Browser type and version
  • Pages visited and time spent
  • Referring website

Google AdSense and Advertising Cookies

TheModelSays uses Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website and other websites on the internet.

Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet. You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google's Ad Settings. Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalised advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

For more information on how Google uses data when you use our site, please visit: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services .

Third-Party Services

Our site uses the following third-party services that may process data:

  • Vercel — our hosting provider. See Vercel Privacy Policy.
  • Railway — our backend API provider. See Railway Privacy Policy.
  • Google AdSense — advertising. See Google Privacy Policy.

Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device. TheModelSays does not set its own cookies. Google AdSense and any other advertising partners may set cookies as described above. You can control cookies through your browser settings or via the opt-out links provided in the advertising section above.

Data Retention

Since we do not collect personal data directly, we have no user data to retain or delete. Any data processed by third-party services (Vercel, Railway, Google) is governed by their respective privacy policies.

Children's Privacy

TheModelSays is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the site after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, you can reach us via our TikTok or YouTube channels.
